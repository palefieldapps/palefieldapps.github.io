Sprachschatz – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026
Developer: palefield.apps
Contact: palefield.apps@gmail.com

Sprachschatz ("the app") is an offline German–English dictionary for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: the dictionary works on your device, there are no user accounts, and I do not run any servers that collect your personal data. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, for advertising and for purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your own device. It is not sent to me or to anyone else:

  • your favourite words and any folders you create
  • your recently viewed words (history)
  • your settings (dark mode, text size, spelling style)
  • whether you have purchased ad removal

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app in your Android settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one small banner advertisement, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. I do not receive this information from Google in a form that identifies you.

  • Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool. You can review or change your choice later at any time under About → Ad privacy options in the app (this option appears where it is required).
  • Android settings: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites – Google's advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

If you purchase ad removal (see below), the app no longer shows ads.

3. In-app purchase (Google Play)

The app offers a one-time purchase that removes the banner ad. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details. The app only keeps a local record on your device that the purchase was made, and can ask Google Play to restore it if you reinstall the app or use a new device ("Restore purchases").

4. Microphone and speech

Voice input. If you use voice input, the app asks for microphone permission. Audio is not recorded or stored by the app. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service built into your device (which, depending on your device, may be provided by Google) and is subject to that service's own privacy policy. You can deny or withdraw the permission at any time in your Android settings and still use the rest of the app.

Pronunciation. When you tap the play button to hear a word, the app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (which, depending on your device, may be provided by Google). The app does not collect or store what is spoken, and the engine is subject to its own privacy policy.

5. Information I do not collect

  • I do not require an account, and I do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • I do not sell your personal information.
  • The app does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

6. Dictionary content

The dictionary data comes from Wiktionary (via the Wiktextract project) and is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and the GNU Free Documentation License. Credits and links are shown in the app under About. The dictionary is stored in the app and looked up offline.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the effective date shown above.

9. Contact
